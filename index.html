Privacy Policy for Chroma Sort Color Ball Puzzle

Last Updated: August 03, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Chroma Sort Color Ball Puzzle ("Application") for mobile devices.

1. What Information Does the Application Obtain and How Is It Used?

User Provided Information

The Application does not require registration to play offline. However, if you choose to secure your game progress via Google Play Games, you can link an email address and set an optional nickname. This authentication data is securely handled via Firebase Authentication.

Automatically Collected Information

The Application may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to: the type of mobile device you use, your mobile device's unique device ID, IP address, operating system, and crash reports.

2. Third-Party Services & SDK Declarations

We use verified third-party SDKs to secure your progression and measure application health. These services collect and process data in compliance with Google Play Policies:

  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore: Used to safely store and synchronize your game data. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: Used to log crashes automatically so we can fix bugs faster, and to understand general gameplay behavior. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Games Services: Used for basic app functionality and leaderboards/achievements.

3. Data Retention Policy & Managing Your Information

We will retain User Provided data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter.

4. Account and Data Deletion Request (Google Play Compliance)

In compliance with Google Play's User Data policy regarding account deletion:

  • In-App Deletion: You can request the deletion of your progress and account data by contacting us directly.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you wish to delete your account, linked email address, and all associated progress data stored in our servers (Firebase) without installing the application, please send an explicit deletion request from your registered email address to rafaelmeneghelo@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  • All associated user data and progression records will be permanently deleted from our databases (Firebase Auth and Firestore) within 7 business days of request confirmation.

5. Children’s Privacy

We do not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that his or her child has provided us with information without their consent, he or she should contact us at rafaelmeneghelo@gmail.com. We will delete such information from our files within a reasonable time.

6. Security

We are concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Please be aware that, although we endeavor to provide reasonable security, no security system can prevent all potential security breaches.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, please contact us at: rafaelmeneghelo@gmail.com
