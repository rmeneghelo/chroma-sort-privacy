Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Chroma Sort Color Ball Puzzle app for mobile devices. The Application is intended for use "AS IS".

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not explicitly request, collect, or require you to provide any personal data directly to play offline. However, the Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, provide game features, or report crashes.

Link to the privacy policy of third-party service providers used by the Application:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Games
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics

2. Log Data

We want to inform you that whenever you use our Service, in a case of an error in the app we collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

3. Account and Data Deletion Request (Google Play Compliance)

In compliance with Google Play's User Data policy regarding account deletion:

  • In-App Deletion: You can request the deletion of your progress and account data by contacting us directly.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you wish to delete your account, linked email address, and all associated progress data stored in our servers (Firebase) without installing the application, please send an explicit deletion request from your registered email address to rafaelmeneghelo@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  • All associated user data and progression records will be permanently deleted from our databases (Firebase Auth and Firestore) within 7 business days of request confirmation.

4. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to do necessary actions.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
