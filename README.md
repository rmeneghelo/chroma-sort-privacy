# Chroma Sort Color Ball Puzzle - Privacy Policy

This repository is dedicated exclusively to hosting the official Privacy Policy for the **Chroma Sort Color Ball Puzzle** mobile game, published on the Google Play Store. 

You can view the live Privacy Policy page here: 
👉 **[Chroma Sort Privacy Policy](https://rmeneghelo.github.io/chroma-sort-privacy/)**

## Overview
We prioritize user privacy and transparency. The game does not actively ask for or collect personal data. However, to ensure a stable and bug-free experience without intrusive ads, the app relies on minimal standard third-party services, such as:
- **Firebase Analytics:** To understand general gameplay behavior and improve user experience.
- **Firebase Crashlytics:** To log crashes automatically so we can fix bugs faster.
- **Google Play Services:** For basic app functionality and Google Play Games integration.

The full details regarding the usage of these services are available on the policy page linked above.
