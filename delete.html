Account and Data Deletion Request

How to delete your data

If you wish to request the deletion of your account and any associated data from the Chroma Sort Color Ball Puzzle game, please send an email to our support team.

Email: rafaelmeneghelo@gmail.com

What to include in your email:

  • Subject: Data Deletion Request - Chroma Sort
  • Body: Please provide your Google Play Games username or the email address associated with your account so we can locate and remove your data.

What happens next?

Once we receive your request, we will manually delete your account and wipe all associated gameplay data, crash reports, and analytics linked to your account from our Firebase servers. This process may take up to 7 business days.

Please note that this action is irreversible, and you will lose all your progress in the game.
